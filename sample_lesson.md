# Photosynthesis and Respiration: Plants and Food Production
**Curriculum Standard:** `NS-G7-T1-01`  
**Class Size:** 45 Students | **Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Verification Score:** 100%  

## Verified Learning Outcomes
- Explain the process of photosynthesis: plants manufacture glucose using chlorophyll, sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water.
- Identify oxygen as the by-product of photosynthesis.
- Differentiate between photosynthesis (energy storage) and respiration (energy release in plants and animals).

## Large Class Management (40+ Students)
- **Seating:** Standard dual-desk rows arranged into 'Shoulder Partners' (pairs) and 'Pod of 4' (turn-around desks) for instant peer-checking without moving furniture.
- **Attention Cue:** Teacher call-and-response rhythm: 'Eyes on me in 3... 2... 1 (Pencils down, eyes front)'. Avoids shouting over 40+ learners.
- **Rapid Formative Check:** Choral response & Finger Voting (Hold up 1, 2, 3, or 4 fingers against the chest to signal option choice simultaneously).
