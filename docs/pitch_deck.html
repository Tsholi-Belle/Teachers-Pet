02 • The Solution

Teacher's Pet: A True Autonomous Pedagogical Colleague

An autonomous multi-agent system that collaborates to deliver structured, verified classroom packs in seconds.

PL

Planner Agent

Interprets simple teacher outcomes, maps pedagogical time-blocks (Hook, Instruction, Guided Practice, Formative Check), and assigns worker tasks.

WK

Worker Agents (Grounding, Lesson & Exercise)

Connects strictly to accredited curriculum databases (CAPS/NGSS), generates 3-panel chalkboard layouts, and produces paper-saving matching sheets.

RV

Reviewer Agent

Audits every claim. Ensures 100% outcome alignment, tests matching key uniqueness, and blocks unverified links or unrealistic tech requirements.

05

Self-Correction Engine

Catches errors automatically and loops work back to workers for repair before final delivery, guaranteeing teachers never receive broken materials.

03 • Truth & Integrity

Why "Does Not Lie" is Non-Negotiable in Education

Teachers face official department inspections. Fictional citations or inaccurate science concepts damage student futures.

✅

Accredited Standard Whitelist

Every generated outcome is locked to authenticated syllabus codes (e.g., CAPS NS-G7-T1-01 or NGSS MS-LS1-6). If a standard cannot be verified, it is flagged and blocked.

✅

Verified Source Repository

Only accredited departmental books (DBE, Sasol Inzalo, Siyavula) are permitted. AI is strictly barred from inventing blog links or fictional citations.

✅

Mathematical & Pedagogical Soundness

Matching exercises are tested against ambiguity algorithms: every term has exactly one distinct matching definition, eliminating unfair grading traps.

04 • Pragmatic Pedagogy

Engineered for the Realities of 40+ Learner Classes

How Teacher's Pet solves overcrowding without requiring high-tech budgets.

📄 Paper-Saver Layouts

Generates 2-up half-sheet formats (1 A4 shared between 2 learners = only 23 photocopies for 45 kids) or direct chalkboard copying for zero school expenditure.

👥 Zero-Chaos Desk Grouping

Structures cooperative learning around "Shoulder Partners" (desk neighbors) and "Pods of 4" (turning front desks around) without moving furniture across a crowded room.

🗣️ 90-Second Whole-Class Checking

Embeds choral unison call-and-response and chest-finger voting routines, enabling teachers to gauge 45 kids' comprehension immediately without 2 hours of evening red-pen marking.

📝 3-Panel Chalkboard System

Standardizes chalkboard layout into Panel 1 (Date & Vocabulary), Panel 2 (Concept Diagram), and Panel 3 (Classwork Matching Exercise) for clean student copying.
